Notes
— Lobby is the only light screen on mobile; its dark header keeps the table's voice at the top.
— End cards are bottom sheets, not centred dialogs — both actions sit inside thumb reach.
— Waiting screen is a real screen, not a spinner: the standing wager and ledger stay readable.
— Palifico appears once per triggering round, dismissed with one tap, then the normal bid sheet returns.
— Reconnect keeps your dice face-down: the held hand is shown as blanks, never revealed on a drop.
