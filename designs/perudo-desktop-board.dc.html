What changes from mobile
Element
Desktop treatment
Bid sheet
Becomes a permanent 340px right rail — no rise/fall; when it isn't your turn the rail shows the status card instead of controls
Ledger chip
Expands into a persistent left column with live bid history and a dice-remaining bar per seat
Arc
Widens to a full ellipse; seats grow to 62px (72px active) and carry pip counts under the name
Haptics
Replaced by hover tints from the accent ramp plus keyboard shortcuts (Enter / L)
Your hand
