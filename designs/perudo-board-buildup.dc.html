Why this order
Hand first, because it is the only thing on screen that is yours and it never leaves. Opponents next as anonymous positions — the arc has to hold its shape before it holds identities. The wager third, because it is meaningless without someone to have made it. Round, count and names last: they are labels on a board that already reads.
Reserved space
The header band keeps its 44px in step 1 and 2 even while empty, and every seat column is 84px wide from the start, so names drop in without pushing avatars. The mat's 300px block is fixed too — the wager token appears inside space already held for it.
Not here yet
No bid sheet, no roll button, no turn ring, no eliminated seat, no touch-to-look blur on the hand. Those are the next layer and belong on top of a board that is already stable.
