Notes for the build
— Lobby is the only light-ground screen; the dark header keeps continuity with the table.
— Entrance seats fill in place: the dashed empty card becomes a filled avatar card, no relayout.
— Both end cards reuse one shell: icon + kicker + heading, body line, three stat tiles, actions.
— Win card is the only place the accent gradient badge appears; keep it rare so it reads as a prize.
— The lost die animates out of the cup, then the card rises 350ms behind it.
— Elimination (last die lost) is the lose card with "You are out" copy and a Watch action.
