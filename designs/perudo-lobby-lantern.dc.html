One room, not two screens
The dark band and cream card become a single lantern-lit space, so this reads as the same place as the board. The crew rows stay light-on-dark and the host row takes a brass edge instead of a separate tint block.
The code becomes shareable
"Share the code below" with no way to share it is the biggest miss. It moves onto the same cream, brass-edged plate the wager token uses on the board, at 36px, with Share invite and Copy attached — one tap into the group chat, still large enough to read aloud.
The blank space becomes the ask
Three hundred empty pixels turn into the seats that are still open, with the next one breathing. The count reads 2 of 6 · 0 ready, so you can see both how full the table is and how far off the start you are — and the sentence about every player being ready is replaced by the rule under the button, where the decision happens.
Next
Dissolve this room into the board when the match begins — the seats filled here become the seats they play in, so nobody re-orients. From here the flow runs on to the cast screen.
Perudo · entry screen
Lobby, rethought in lantern light
The current screen asks for a room code before you have said whether you are joining one, dresses the primary action in a tint that reads disabled, and blocks the top of the card with a connecting spinner. Three replacements below, all 390×844.
What is wrong now
Two text fields and two buttons on arrival, and the field you need depends on the button you have not pressed. "Leave blank to start a new table" asks you to reason about emptiness.
Contrast
Both buttons sit at accent-300 strength on a pale card, so the primary reads greyed out and the secondary reads unavailable. Neither invites a first tap.
Mood
A white card on lilac is nobody's tavern. The board this leads into is lantern-lit and dark — the entrance should already be in the room.
