Header chips
36px tall, 13.2px inset, 12.5px/600 Figtree. Phase name only — no engine enum. Ledger chip is the sole route to bid history.
Arc seat
84px wide, 4.4px internal gap. Identity is a 54×54 rounded-rectangle avatar tile (radius 17) — deliberately larger than any table die carrying the carved mark placeholder — auto-assigned per seat, real avatars to follow. Active seat: 60px, 3px brass ring. Name 12.5px/600 sits under it; dice counts are omitted — the client renders them.
Opening slots
The slot sits in each seat’s own column, directly in front of the name and facing the mat — so a result reads as that player’s, radially, with no legend. 40px, dashed while waiting; the die replaces the dash in place, so nothing reflows when results land. Winner slot gets a 3px brass ring and 44px, the only size change — it stays smaller than the avatar above it, so identity outranks result.
Tie state
The “Wagers first” pill sits above the winner’s avatar, never over the mat. Tie copy only, in the caption slot: “Anne and Mateo tied, casting again”. No colour, no movement — the slots simply return to dashed.
Your cast
No cup here — the opening die is public, so it sits bare on the mat at 66px. Button 56px tall, full gutter width, brass fill. Shake or tap both roll; caption states both.

Live frame
Tap the brass chevron to run bidding → reveal → round loss. 34×64 tab, flush to the frame edge — prototype affordance only, not a shipped control.
Turn state, hue-free
Active seat = 3px solid brass ring plus name at 14px/700 (others 12.5px/600). Recognisable with colour removed; the lantern glow above the arc dims slowly as the turn clock runs, never showing digits.
Wager token
Sits on the mat in front of whoever bid. Quantity 38px Caprasimo, die 50px — both readable at arm's length. Spent tokens from earlier in the round drop to 30% and shrink to a chip; full history only in the Ledger.
Bid sheet
Rises only on your turn. Hand strip pinned at its top, unblurred while the sheet is open. Steppers 58×44 with 44px+ hit slop; face die 62px. Suggestion pills 44px tall, horizontally scrollable, disabled ones drop the label.
Actions
Place wager is the only filled button on screen; Call liar is the only outlined one — the danger cue survives greyscale. Both 56px tall. Heavy haptic on Call liar, light tick per stepper step.

Two arcs of six
Above eight players the arc splits: six per row, the back row inset and dimmed slightly to hold depth. Seats narrow to 58px, cups to 32×24, names truncate with an ellipsis.
What gives
The status line is cut and avatar tiles drop to 42px; the row tightens rather than shrinking type. Roll and turn state fall back to the ring and the tick alone.
Wager token
Shrinks one step (32px quantity, 44px die) and moves to the mat's centre line rather than in front of a seat — twelve seats leave no room in front of any of them. Shown mid-match at 50 dice; round 1 would show five pips on every seat.
